Platani Seaside Villa

Nature is all around, welcome to your private escape located steps from the sea.

A private sanctuary in Eastern Crete

Rooted in Crete.
Open to the Aegean.

Welcome to Platani Seaside Villa, a peaceful seaside retreat where authentic Cretan hospitality meets the timeless beauty of the Mediterranean. Surrounded by nature and bathed in warm island light, the villa offers uninterrupted sea views, complete privacy and the welcoming feeling of a true home.

Wake to the sound of the waves, enjoy slow mornings on the balcony and spend sun-filled days only a few steps from the sea. As evening arrives, the spacious terrace, outdoor kitchen and barbecue create a beautiful setting for shared meals beneath the Cretan sky — an unhurried experience designed for reconnection and memories that remain long after your stay.

02 · The beach

Your Morning Begins by the Water

Platani Beach is only a few steps from the villa. Its clear water and peaceful shoreline invite early swims, unhurried afternoons and evenings shaped by the changing colours of the Aegean. The setting feels wonderfully secluded, yet Sitia remains only a short drive away.

  • Beachfront
  • Sea View
  • Beach Just 20 m Away
  • Private Balcony
  • Sun Terrace
03 · Outdoor evenings

Evenings Beneath the Cretan Sky

As the afternoon light softens, the garden becomes the heart of the villa. Choose fresh local ingredients, prepare dinner together at the outdoor kitchen and gather around the table with a glass of wine. There is no need to hurry — stay outside as the sky fills with stars, the stone garden begins to glow and the gentle sound of the fountains accompanies conversations that last late into the warm Cretan night.
